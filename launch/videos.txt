FIVE SHORT-FORM VIDEO SCRIPTS (each 45 seconds or less; faceless: screen recordings + captions + voiceover)

HOW TO MAKE THEM
- Record the live site (after the first data run) on a phone-sized browser window, 1080x1920.
- A friend reads the voiceover; burn in captions. No music needed; if used, keep it quiet.
- Post to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok. Suggested (heuristic, not measured): weekday
  evenings, 6 to 9 p.m. Central, when students scroll after classes.
- Numbers in brackets come from https://firstsilicon.pages.dev/data/meta.json on the day you record.
- End card for all: firstsilicon.pages.dev (put the link in the bio or description with ?ref=shorts).
- Never show fictional/demo data; record the real site only.
