POST
Hardware internships are a small slice of the big internship lists (the popular Summer 2027 list on GitHub counted 275 hardware engineering roles next to 681 software engineering ones on Sept 23), and hardware postings rarely say whether a first- or second-year student can apply.

So I built First Silicon, a free list of hardware internships and co-ops that updates every day from companies' public job boards: https://firstsilicon.pages.dev/?ref=linkedin

Each role is labeled with:
- its discipline (embedded, FPGA/ASIC, analog, RF, power, PCB, test, controls, semiconductor process, photonics)
- whether the posting welcomes freshmen and sophomores
- US citizenship, ITAR/export-control and clearance requirements

Right now it lists [ROLE_COUNT] roles at [EMPLOYER_COUNT] employers, and [FS_COUNT] of them mention freshman/sophomore eligibility. There's also a calendar of programs open before junior year (DOE SULI, NASA, NIST SURF, NSF REU).

If you're an EE or CE underclassman, or you mentor one, I'd love to hear what's missing.

Built by Mahir Patel, an electrical-engineering student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.
