POST
I set out to build a list of hardware internships that freshmen and sophomores can apply to. The first live run said that list would have 4 roles on it.

On September 24, First Silicon read 15,969 postings on 88 hardware companies' job boards. 191 were hardware internships or co-ops, at 34 companies. Of those:
- 4 say freshmen or sophomores can apply
- 15 ask for junior standing or later
- 172 don't state a class year at all

So First Silicon is now every hardware internship, labeled: https://firstsilicon.pages.dev/?ref=linkedin

Each role is tagged with its discipline (test, PCB, controls, embedded, analog, FPGA/ASIC, RF, power, photonics, semiconductor process), its class year, and any US citizenship, ITAR/export-control or clearance requirement. It updates every morning from the companies' own job boards. There's also a calendar of programs open before junior year (DOE SULI, NASA, NIST SURF, NSF REU).

For students: if a posting doesn't say juniors/seniors or a graduation date you can't meet, apply.
For engineers who write internship postings: one sentence on which class years can apply would answer the question for a lot of students.

Numbers are from September 24 and change daily. Built by Mahir Patel, an electrical-engineering student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.
