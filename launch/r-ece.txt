TITLE
I built a daily list of hardware internships labeled by class year. Of 191 postings, 4 say freshmen/sophomores can apply and 172 don't mention class year at all

BODY
I'm an electrical engineering student at UIUC. I started out building a list of hardware internships that freshmen and sophomores can apply to. The first live run (September 24) read 15,969 postings on 88 hardware companies' job boards and found 191 hardware internships and co-ops at 34 companies. By class year:

- 4 say freshmen or sophomores can apply
- 15 ask for junior standing or later, a graduate degree, or a graduation date that rules out underclassmen
- 172 don't mention class year at all

A list of 4 isn't much use, so First Silicon now lists every hardware internship and labels it: https://firstsilicon.pages.dev/?ref=r-ece

If you're a freshman or sophomore, the 172 are the ones to read. Apply unless the posting says juniors/seniors or a graduation date you can't meet.

What each role shows:
- Discipline: test/validation (82 today), PCB/hardware (53), controls/robotics (39), embedded/firmware (33), analog (18), FPGA/RTL/ASIC (17), RF (17), plus power, photonics and semiconductor process. A role can have more than one.
- Class year: Fr/So friendly, Juniors+ or "not stated", with a "Why these labels?" excerpt from the posting so you can check it.
- US citizenship, US-person/ITAR and clearance flags. 79 of the 191 have none, and one checkbox hides the rest. All 4 of today's Fr/So postings carry US-person/ITAR language.
- Term, internship vs co-op, and location.

There's also a calendar of programs whose official pages list no junior-standing requirement (DOE SULI, NASA OSTEM, NIST SURF, NSF REU and a couple of national labs).

Caveats: the labels come from pattern matching, so some are wrong. It only reads companies on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby, so a lot of big semiconductor and defense companies on Workday aren't in it yet. The numbers above are from September 24 and change every morning.

It's free and there's no sign-up. If a label is wrong or your target company is missing, tell me in the comments.
