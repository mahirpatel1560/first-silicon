TITLE
I built a free list of hardware internships that shows which ones say freshmen and sophomores can apply

BODY
I'm an electrical-engineering student at UIUC. The big internship lists are mostly software, and hardware postings rarely make it obvious whether a first- or second-year student can apply. (On Sept 23 the popular Summer 2027 list on GitHub counted 275 hardware engineering roles next to 681 software engineering ones: https://github.com/SimplifyJobs/Summer2027-Internships)

So I made First Silicon: https://firstsilicon.pages.dev/?ref=r-ece

What it does:
- Reads hardware companies' public job boards once a day (Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby) and keeps the internships and co-ops that look like hardware work.
- Tags each role by discipline: embedded/firmware, FPGA/RTL/ASIC, analog, RF, power electronics, PCB, test, controls/robotics, semiconductor process and photonics.
- Labels class year from the posting text. "Fr/So friendly" means it mentions freshmen, sophomores, rising juniors, all class years or 2029/2030 graduation. "Juniors+" means it asks for junior standing or a grad degree.
- Flags US citizenship, ITAR/export control and clearance requirements, with a filter to hide them.
- Has a calendar of programs whose official pages list no junior-standing requirement (DOE SULI, NASA OSTEM, NIST SURF, NSF REU and a couple of national labs).

As of [DATE]: [ROLE_COUNT] open roles at [EMPLOYER_COUNT] employers, and [FS_COUNT] of them mention freshman/sophomore eligibility.

Caveats: the labels come from pattern matching, so they can be wrong. Every role links to the employer's posting and has a "Why these labels?" excerpt so you can check. It only covers companies on those three job-board systems, so many big semiconductor and defense companies aren't in it yet.

It's free and there's no sign-up. If a label is wrong or your target company is missing, tell me in the comments. What filter would make this more useful to you?
