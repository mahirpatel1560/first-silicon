# First Silicon: hardware internships for freshmen and sophomores

Hardware, EE, embedded and semiconductor internships and co-ops pulled daily from public employer job boards (Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby), labeled by discipline, class-year signal and citizenship/export flags. Filter the list at **[firstsilicon.pages.dev](https://firstsilicon.pages.dev/?ref=github)**.

**Status:** the first automatic data run has not happened yet. The table fills in after the daily GitHub Action runs.

> Labels are generated automatically from posting text and can be wrong. Always read the full posting on the employer's site before applying. Closed roles are removed on the next daily run.

## Legend

- **Class year**: `Fr/So` = the posting mentions first-year, sophomore, rising-junior or all-class-year eligibility (or a 2029-2030 graduation date); `Jr+` = it asks for junior/senior standing, graduation by 2027-2028, or a graduate degree; blank = not stated (often still open to sophomores, so read it).
- **Flags**: `US citizen`, `US person / PR`, `ITAR / export`, `Clearance`, `No visa sponsorship` are detected from the posting text. No flag does not guarantee there is no restriction.
- **Posted**: the employer's publish date when the job board provides one, otherwise the date we first saw it `(seen)`. `new` = first seen in the last 7 days.

## Early-career programs worth a look

Government and national-lab programs whose official pages list no junior-standing requirement, each checked on that page. Details: [the programs calendar](https://firstsilicon.pages.dev/programs/).

- **September 30, 2026**: [Community College Internships (CCI)](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/cci) (U.S. Department of Energy, Office of Science (Workforce Development for Teachers and Scientists)), Spring 2027 term
- **September 30, 2026**: [Science Undergraduate Laboratory Internships (SULI)](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/suli) (U.S. Department of Energy, Office of Science (Workforce Development for Teachers and Scientists)), Spring 2027 term
- **January 31, 2027**: [Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship (SURF)](https://www.nist.gov/surf) (National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)), Summer 2027
- **February 26, 2027**: [NASA OSTEM Internships](https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/internship-programs/) (NASA Office of STEM Engagement), Summer 2027
- [Research Experiences for Undergraduates (REU) Sites](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/reu-research-experiences-undergraduates) (U.S. National Science Foundation): Set by each REU site. Students apply to sites directly, not to NSF; use the NSF search to find electrical and computer engineering sites.
- [Sandia National Laboratories internships and co-ops](https://www.sandia.gov/careers/career-possibilities/students-and-postdocs/internships-co-ops/) (Sandia National Laboratories (operated for the U.S. Department of Energy)): No fixed deadline on the page: apply to current openings. Summer internships run 10 to 12 weeks (typically May to August); co-ops run 3 to 8 months during the academic year.
- [Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory student internships](https://www.llnl.gov/join-our-team/careers/students) (Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory): Summer positions are generally posted September through February; phone interviews happen December through April.

## How this list is made

A scheduled GitHub Action calls the public job-board APIs of the configured employer boards once a day, keeps internship and co-op postings that look like hardware work, and removes roles that disappear. Only titles, locations, dates, a link to the original posting and a short (at most 300-character) excerpt used to explain the labels are stored. Details: https://firstsilicon.pages.dev/how-it-works/

Suggest an employer or report a wrong label by opening an issue, or use the contact form on the site.
